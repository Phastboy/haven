# Stage 0.6 saved offer lifecycle

A saved offer whose final image is removed remains in the person's saved collection, but its row is shown as **Offer no longer available** with no public link or offer details. This preserves the person's saved intent while honoring the existing rule that offers without images are private.

Deleting an offer cascades to its saved rows. Deleted offers therefore leave every saved collection, and no tombstone or historical offer data is retained.
